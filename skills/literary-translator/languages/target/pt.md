# Portuguese as the target language (`pt`)

> Status: first draft. Native review welcome (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

Read this file when translating **into** Portuguese, together with the source-language file. Checker option: `--to pt`.

## Variant

European or Brazilian Portuguese: one choice for the book, recorded in the brief. Vocabulary, some spellings, pronouns and the forms of address change.

## Dialogue and punctuation

- Dialogue with the travessão (—).
- « » (Portugal) or “ ” (Brazil) for quotations.
- The script flags a hyphen or en dash instead of the travessão, and dialogue in quotation marks.

## Forms of address

tu / você / o senhor, a senhora: their use differs between Portugal and Brazil. Record in the brief who uses which form with whom, and where it changes.
