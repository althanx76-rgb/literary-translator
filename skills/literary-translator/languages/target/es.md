# Spanish as the target language (`es`)

> Status: first draft. Native review welcome (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

Read this file when translating **into** Spanish, together with the source-language file. Checker option: `--to es`.

## Variant

Spain or Latin America: one choice for the book, recorded in the brief. Vocabulary, idioms and the plural "you" change (vosotros in Spain, ustedes in Latin America); in some countries the voseo replaces tú.

## Dialogue and punctuation

- Dialogue with the raya (—), without a space after the opening dash: —Ya voy —dijo—. ¿Vienes?
- Opening ¿ and ¡ always.
- « » for quotations.
- The script flags questions and exclamations without the opening mark, a raya followed by a space, a hyphen or en dash instead of the raya, and dialogue in quotation marks.

## Dialogue tags

Varied dialogue verbs are accepted in Spanish: follow the original.

## Forms of address

tú / usted (and vosotros / ustedes, see Variant). Record in the brief who uses which form with whom, and where it changes.

## Swearing

Religious swearing has a force in Spanish comparable to Italian blasphemy: it can often be rendered within the same area.
